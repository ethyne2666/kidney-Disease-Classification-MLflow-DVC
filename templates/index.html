Understanding the kidney and kidney tumors

What the kidneys do

You have two bean-shaped kidneys, each about the size of a fist, below your rib cage on either side of your spine. They filter your blood, remove waste and extra fluid as urine, and help control blood pressure and the balance of salts in your body.

What a kidney tumor is

A kidney tumor is an abnormal growth of cells in the kidney. Some are benign and some are cancerous. The most common kidney cancer in adults is renal cell carcinoma. Many tumors are found by chance on a scan done for another reason.

Signs to watch for

Early tumors often cause no symptoms. Later signs can include blood in the urine, a lump or lasting pain in the side, unexplained weight loss and tiredness. See a doctor if you notice any of these.

Why CT scans are used

A CT scan shows the kidney in detail, so doctors can see the size and position of a growth. A specialist makes the final diagnosis, often with further tests. This model only helps to flag scans that need a closer look.
